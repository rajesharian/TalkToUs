Dr. Lalit Mohan Pant

Founder · MA Psychology · PhD in Positive Psychology · MBA (HRM)

Dr. Lalit Mohan Pant holds an MA in Psychology, a PhD in Positive Psychology, an MBA (HRM), UGC NET in HRM, a B.Ed, and a Diploma in Youth Mental Health & Peer Counselling from LGBRIMH.

He brings 21 years of experience as an academician and mentor. He has edited 3 books, authored 16 units for Uttarakhand Open University and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Open University Hyderabad, and published 26 research papers in international journals.

As the founder of Talk2Us, Dr. Pant is dedicated to providing a safe, compassionate and judgment-free space where individuals can share their thoughts, feelings and struggles openly — helping people navigate life's challenges, gain clarity and rediscover strength within.
