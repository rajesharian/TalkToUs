FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Still have something on your mind? Reach out via the contact form and we'll get back to you.

Who is Talk2Us for? +

Individuals, students, families and professionals in Haldwani and beyond — anyone looking for a safe, non-judgmental space to talk through what they're facing.

How does a session work? +

You book a slot between 10 AM and 6 PM, and meet one-on-one with one of our trained counsellors in a confidential setting.

Are sessions confidential? +

Yes. Confidentiality is one of our core values — everything you share stays within a safe, trusted space.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking? +

Yes, just reach out to us by phone or email and we'll help you find a new time.

How do donations support free sessions? +

Donations help us offer free or low-cost sessions to students and job-seekers who might not otherwise be able to access support.

Do you offer sessions in languages other than English? +

Yes — sessions are also available in Hindi, based on what's most comfortable for you.
