Er. Anurag Bhatt

B.Tech (CSE), M.Tech (CSE) · Technologist & Academician

An accomplished academician and technologist currently serving as Assistant Director, Computer IT at Uttarakhand Open University under the Government of Uttarakhand, with over eighteen years of academic and industry experience. His areas of expertise include Artificial Intelligence, Data Mining & Analytics and Cloud Computing.

He was awarded two Design Patents by the Government of India (2026) and is the recipient of the prestigious Young Scientist Award (2024) conferred by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST). He has authored over thirty-eight research papers in reputed national and international journals and conferences, and has contributed to publications with Springer, IEEE, and Scopus-indexed platforms.

He is actively associated with scholarly journals as a reviewer and author, and has significantly contributed to digital education initiatives through SWAYAM and SWAYAM-Prabha MOOCs.
